California permit test study guide

Everything the California knowledge test for a car (Class C) permit covers, explained in plain words, lesson by lesson, from the California Driver's Handbook (DL 600 English, rev. 6/2025). Each lesson names the handbook pages it covers and ends with the practice test, 179 questions with explanations.

How to use this study guide

  1. Read the lessons in order; together they cover the handbook from getting your permit to what to do after a collision.
  2. After each lesson, take the practice test in practice mode: every answer shows a short explanation and the handbook page.
  3. Reread the handbook pages behind every question you miss, then try exam mode until you pass comfortably.
  4. Learn the shapes and colors of road signs with our road signs practice test.

The lessons are our own explanations, not a copy of the handbook and not the questions of the DMV test. The California DMV handbook and the state’s Vehicle Code are the final word if anything differs.

The lessons

  1. Lesson 1

    California Permit Study Guide: Getting Your Permit, the DMV Tests and Driving Basics

    How a California instruction permit and license work: documents, steps and teen rules, the vision, knowledge and behind-the-wheel tests, then the basics every new driver must know about steering, signaling, the horn and headlights.

    9 min read · Handbook sections 1 to 5, pages 1 to 12

  2. Lesson 2

    California Permit Study Guide: Lane Markings, Turns, Merging, Passing and Parking

    Handbook Section 6 in plain words: what each lane line means, which lane to use, how to turn right, left and around, when U-turns are banned, how to merge and pass, how to park on a hill, curb colors and where you may never park.

    14 min read · Handbook section 6, pages 13 to 31

  3. Lesson 3

    California Permit Study Guide: Signals, Signs, Right-of-Way and the Rules of the Road

    Handbook Section 7 in plain words: every traffic light and arrow, the signs and their shapes, right-of-way at intersections and roundabouts, sharing the road with trucks, bikes and emergency vehicles, special speed limits, and how points and suspensions work.

    16 min read · Handbook section 7, pages 33 to 56

  4. Lesson 4

    California Permit Study Guide: Safe Driving, Alcohol and Drugs, Insurance and Collisions

    Handbook Sections 8 to 10 in plain words: scanning and following distance, night, glare, skids and bad weather, seat belts and child seats, the Basic Speed Law, emergencies, phones, DUI and BAC rules, insurance and collisions.

    16 min read · Handbook sections 8 to 10, pages 57 to 78
