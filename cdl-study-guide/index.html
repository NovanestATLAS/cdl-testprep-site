How to use this study guide

  1. Find out which tests you need: CDL Classes, Endorsement Codes and Restriction Codes explains classes A, B and C and every endorsement.
  2. Read the lessons for your tests in order. Every CDL applicant needs Part 1; add the parts for air brakes, Class A and your endorsements.
  3. After each lesson, take the matching practice test in practice mode, then reread the manual section of every question you miss.
  4. Before test day, switch to exam mode and aim well above the 80% pass mark. Our guide How to Study for the CDL Knowledge Tests has a full plan.

The lessons are our own summaries, not a copy of the manual and not the questions of the real test. Your state publishes its own CDL manual; it is the final word if anything differs.

Part 1: General Knowledge (every CDL applicant)

The General Knowledge test is the one every applicant takes. These lessons cover sections 2 and 3 of the manual: inspecting your vehicle, driving safely and carrying cargo.

  1. Lesson 1

    CDL General Knowledge: Vehicle Inspection, Basic Control and Shifting

    The first part of the General Knowledge test: the inspections every driver must do, the defects that put a vehicle out of service, and the basics of starting, steering, stopping, backing and shifting a heavy vehicle.

    12 min read · Manual sections 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3 · Practice: General Knowledge test

  2. Lesson 2

    CDL General Knowledge: Seeing, Signaling, Speed and Space Management

    How a professional driver sees, signals and manages speed and space: the 12 to 15 second look-ahead, mirror checks, where to put warning triangles, the three parts of stopping distance, slippery roads, following distance and wide turns.

    15 min read · Manual sections 2.4, 2.5, 2.6 and 2.7 · Practice: General Knowledge test

  3. Lesson 3

    CDL General Knowledge: Seeing Hazards, Distracted and Aggressive Drivers

    How to spot trouble before it becomes an emergency: hazardous roads, the clues other drivers give away, the federal phone and texting rules with their penalties, and how to stay calm around aggressive drivers.

    12 min read · Manual sections 2.8, 2.9 and 2.10 · Practice: General Knowledge test

  4. Lesson 4

    CDL General Knowledge: Night, Fog, Winter, Heat, Railroad Crossings and Mountains

    Driving when conditions turn against you: fatigue and glare at night, fog, winter and summer checks, wet brakes, how to approach and cross railroad tracks, and how to get a heavy vehicle down a mountain without losing the brakes.

    16 min read · Manual sections 2.11, 2.12, 2.13, 2.14, 2.15 and 2.16 · Practice: General Knowledge test

  5. Lesson 5

    CDL General Knowledge: Emergencies, ABS, Skids, Crashes and Fires

    What to do when things go wrong: evasive steering, the two emergency braking methods, failed brakes and tires, what ABS can and cannot do, how to recover from skids, what to do at a crash, and how to fight a truck fire.

    15 min read · Manual sections 2.17, 2.18, 2.19, 2.20 and 2.21 · Practice: General Knowledge test

  6. Lesson 6

    CDL General Knowledge: Alcohol and Drugs, HazMat Basics and Cargo Safety

    The last part of the General Knowledge test: alcohol, drugs and illness, the hazardous materials basics every CDL holder must know, and how to inspect, weigh, balance and secure cargo.

    13 min read · Manual sections 1.3.2, 2.22, 2.23 and 3 · Practice: General Knowledge test

Part 2: Air brakes and combination vehicles

Most trucks with a CDL have air brakes, and a Class A license covers heavy combination vehicles. Each has its own knowledge test.

  1. Lesson 7

    CDL Air Brakes Study Guide: How Air Brakes Work and How to Check Them

    How an air brake system builds and stores pressure, what every gauge and warning means, the air brake checks you must know for the test, and how to brake safely on long downgrades.

    11 min read · Manual section 5 · Practice: Air Brakes test

  2. Lesson 8

    CDL Combination Vehicles Study Guide: Tractor-Trailers, Trailer Air Brakes and Coupling

    What makes tractor-trailers harder to drive, how trailer air brakes and the tractor protection valve work, and how to couple, uncouple and inspect a combination step by step.

    10 min read · Manual section 6 · Practice: Combination Vehicles test

Part 3: Endorsements (H, N, P, S, T)

One lesson per endorsement test: hazardous materials, tank vehicles, passengers, school buses and double or triple trailers.

  1. Lesson 9

    CDL HazMat Endorsement Study Guide: Hazardous Materials Rules

    The rules behind the hazardous materials test in plain words: who is responsible for what, how shipping papers and placards work, how to load and park, and how to handle a leak, a fire or a crash.

    19 min read · Manual sections 1.3.6 and 9 · Practice: HazMat test

  2. Lesson 10

    CDL Doubles and Triples Study Guide: Pulling Two and Three Trailers

    Why doubles and triples are the least stable rigs on the road, how a converter dolly works, the order for coupling and uncoupling twin trailers, and the extra inspection and air brake checks.

    10 min read · Manual section 7 · Practice: Doubles/Triples test

  3. Lesson 11

    CDL Tanker Endorsement Study Guide: Liquid Surge, Baffles and Outage

    Why tank vehicles handle differently, what to check before every load, how surge, bulkheads and baffles change your driving, and how much liquid you can load.

    9 min read · Manual section 8 · Practice: Tank Vehicles test

  4. Lesson 12

    CDL Passenger Endorsement Study Guide: Driving a Bus Safely

    Everything the passenger endorsement test covers: inspecting a bus inside and out, what riders may and may not bring aboard, safe stops and curves, crossings, and the practices that are never allowed.

    9 min read · Manual section 4 · Practice: Passenger test

  5. Lesson 13

    CDL School Bus Endorsement Study Guide: Danger Zones, Loading and Evacuation

    How far the danger zones reach, how to set every mirror, the step-by-step loading and unloading routine, when you must evacuate and where to lead students, and the school bus rules at railroad crossings.

    15 min read · Manual section 10 · Practice: School Bus test

Part 4: The skills test

After the knowledge tests come the vehicle inspection, basic control and on-road tests, taken in the vehicle you want to be licensed for.

  1. Lesson 14

    CDL Pre-Trip Inspection: The Complete Vehicle Inspection Test Checklist

    What the examiner expects at the vehicle inspection test, every item to check from the cab to the coupling, how to explain each one out loud, and the mistakes that end the test.

    10 min read · Manual section 11

  2. Lesson 15

    CDL Skills Test: Basic Control Exercises and the Road Test Explained

    After the pre-trip inspection come the basic control exercises and the on-road test. Here is how each exercise works, how points are counted and what the examiner checks on every turn, merge and crossing.

    8 min read · Manual sections 12 and 13
