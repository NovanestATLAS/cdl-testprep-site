Official sources

CDL Test Prep is not a government website. CDL Test Prep is an independent study site and is not affiliated with any DMV, state agency or the FMCSA. We do not issue permits, licenses or test appointments. Our questions and explanations are our own, written from the official documents below. For anything official, such as rules, fees, test formats or appointments, use these sources and your state’s motor vehicle agency.

Federal CDL rules

  • Commercial Driver’s License Program Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Federal CDL program: who needs a CDL and how to get one from your state.
  • 49 CFR Part 383, Commercial Driver’s License Standards Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) The federal rules for CDL classes, endorsements, knowledge tests and skills tests.
  • 49 CFR 383.21, Number of drivers’ licenses Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) A commercial driver may hold only one driver’s license at a time.
  • 49 CFR 383.25, Commercial learner’s permit (CLP) Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) Who can get a CLP, the CDL holder who must ride along, the P, S and N endorsements allowed on it, how long it lasts and the 14-day wait before the skills test.
  • 49 CFR 383.71, Driver application and certification procedures Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) What you must do before you get a CLP or CDL: age, knowledge tests, the four types of driving you certify and medical certification.
  • 49 CFR 383.93, Endorsements Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) Which vehicles need an endorsement, and which endorsements need a knowledge test only or a skills test too.
  • 49 CFR 383.113, Required skills Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) The three parts of the CDL skills test: vehicle inspection, basic vehicle control and on-road driving.
  • 49 CFR 383.117, Requirements for passenger endorsement Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) What the passenger (P) knowledge test covers, and the skills test in a passenger vehicle.
  • 49 CFR 383.119, Requirements for tank vehicle endorsement Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) What the tank vehicle (N) knowledge test covers.
  • 49 CFR 383.133, Test methods Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) How knowledge and skills tests are given, including the rules on interpreters and on English during the skills test.
  • 49 CFR 383.135, Passing knowledge and skills tests Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) Sets the passing score: at least 80% correct on each CDL knowledge test.
  • 49 CFR 383.141, TSA approval of hazardous materials endorsements Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) A state may issue an H endorsement only after TSA finds that the driver is not a security threat.
  • 49 CFR 383.153, Information on the CLP and CDL documents Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) Sets the standard endorsement codes (H, N, P, S, T, X) and restriction codes printed on every state’s CDL and CLP.
  • 49 CFR 391.11, General qualifications of drivers Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) The basic federal qualifications of interstate commercial drivers: at least 21, English, physically qualified, one license.
  • 49 CFR 391.45, Persons who must be medically examined and certified Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) When a driver needs a DOT medical exam, including a new one when the last certification is more than 24 months old.

Federal agency pages on getting and keeping a CDL

Our guide How to Get a CDL: Federal Requirements Step by Step and the requirements on our endorsement test pages link these pages.

  • ELDT Applicability FMCSA Training Provider Registry Who must complete entry-level driver training (ELDT) and before which test.
  • Frequently Asked Questions: Training Requirements FMCSA Training Provider Registry ELDT details: no minimum hours, the 80% theory assessment, online theory and behind-the-wheel training.
  • National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Where drivers search for a certified medical examiner by city, state or ZIP code.
  • DOT Reminds Commercial Drivers that Physicals Must Now Be Performed by Certified Medical Examiners Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Since May 21, 2014, DOT physicals must be done by an examiner listed on the National Registry.
  • Clearinghouse II and CDL Downgrades FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse Since November 18, 2024, a driver with a prohibited Clearinghouse status loses, or cannot get, a CDL or CLP until the return-to-duty process is done.
  • HAZMAT Endorsement Transportation Security Administration (TSA) How to apply for the TSA security threat assessment that the H endorsement requires.
  • FMCSA ELP Guidance Roadside Policy (MC-SEE-2026-0002) Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) How roadside inspectors check the English language requirement, and when they place a driver out of service.
  • Non-Domiciled CDL 2026 Final Rule FAQs Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Who may get a non-domiciled CLP or CDL under the rule in effect since March 16, 2026.
  • DPS Announces Changes to CDL Knowledge Testing Texas Department of Public Safety Texas gives its CDL and CLP knowledge tests in English only since June 1, 2026.

CDL manuals our CDL tests are written from

Each state agency publishes its own edition of the CDL manual, and every edition below carries a copyright notice from AAMVA (the American Association of Motor Vehicle Administrators). Our CDL practice tests cite the newest edition and check every quote against the others. These are the only CDL manuals we have used: we have not compared the manuals of any other state, so your own state’s manual is the final word.

  • Commercial Driver's License Manual (PDF), Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A) Virginia DMV edition Manual page on the issuer’s website, where the current edition is posted.
  • Commercial Driver's License Manual (PDF), Version March 2025 (document T20964 Rev. 1025) Massachusetts RMV edition
  • Commercial Driver's License Manual (PDF), Version September 9, 2022 Indiana BMV edition
  • CDL Knowledge Exams Virginia Department of Motor Vehicles How Virginia gives the CDL knowledge exams.

Driver handbooks our permit and road signs tests are written from

Road sign standard

  • Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD) Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) The national standard for road signs, signals and pavement markings. The sign drawings on our road signs practice test follow its standard designs.

Find your state’s DMV

You take the knowledge test at your state’s motor vehicle agency, which also publishes your state’s current manual and test rules.
